## Challenge

Overtourism creates a coupled policy problem. More visitors can support employment and public revenue, yet they also increase environmental pressure, infrastructure demand, and conflict with residents. A strict cap may protect the destination while weakening the same local economy the policy is meant to sustain.

## Dynamic model

We built the Sustainable Tourism Dynamics Model as a coupled system of differential equations. It connects tourist flows, environmental indicators, hotel-tax revenue, infrastructure capacity, and policy interventions over time rather than treating them as independent scores.

## Decision layer

Simulation was combined with the Analytic Hierarchy Process to compare policies across social, environmental, and economic objectives. Sensitivity analysis identified influential assumptions and tested whether the recommended intervention remained stable under parameter variation.

## Transfer and results

After developing the framework for Juneau, we tested its transferability in Big Sur. Targeted infrastructure and visitor-management policies increased the modeled sustainability score from **0.33** to **0.74**.
